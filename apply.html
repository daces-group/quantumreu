Who should consider the program?

Undergraduates in computer science, electrical and computer engineering, physics, mathematics, statistics, and related fields who want to explore quantum-network research.

Preparation in at least one area—programming, linear algebra, probability, discrete mathematics, algorithms, circuits, signals, or modern physics—can provide an entry point. Prior quantum coursework is not required.

Students with limited access to research at their home institutions are especially encouraged to explore the program. Preparation and potential are considered in context.

Start thinking about your interests

The proposed application process includes the items below. Final requirements will be published when applications open.

  • A research-interest statement identifying one or more thrusts
  • An unofficial transcript and a preparation inventory, including coursework, projects, employment, or self-study
  • A description of prior research access, anticipated benefit, and next steps
  • One recommendation addressing learning habits, persistence, initiative, and collaboration

The proposed review is holistic, with no strict GPA cutoff and no application fee.

Frequently asked questions

A few things to know.

Do I need quantum computing experience?+

No prior quantum coursework is required. Bridge modules and the first-week bootcamp introduce shared foundations. Different backgrounds offer different entry points into the research.

Can I participate remotely or for part of the summer?+

The program is planned as a full-time, ten-week residential experience at Illinois. Contact program coordination to discuss individual circumstances; alternative arrangements are not advertised as available.

Can I choose my project?+

You can indicate preferred thrusts and interests. Matching considers preparation, mentor capacity, and cohort balance. The final research question and scope will be developed with your mentor.

Will I work on a quantum computer?+

Many projects use theory and classical simulation. Some may use Qiskit or a quantum platform, but specialized hardware access is not required for every project and is not guaranteed.

Are international students eligible?+

The program is designed for students pursuing an undergraduate degree. Final 2027 eligibility requirements, including any citizenship or residency conditions tied to funding, will be published with the application.

What financial support is available?+

$700 per week ($7,000 over ten weeks), fully covered on-campus housing, and travel to and from the Illinois Urbana-Champaign campus. Occasional meals are provided.

How can I apply?+

Applications open December 1, 2026. The planned deadline is January 30, 2027. The application link will appear here when the application opens; contact program coordination with questions.
