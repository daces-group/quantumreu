Project C1 · Thrust 02

Finite-size crossover maps under biased Pauli noise

Enumerate error patterns to see when different small codes exchange their performance advantage.

Exact enumerationStabilizer codesNoise modeling

The question

Where do finite-size logical-error curves cross, and which error patterns explain those crossovers?

Why it matters

Physical devices need not produce X, Y, and Z errors equally often. Small codes make it possible to enumerate errors exactly and test whether familiar code rankings survive a change in the noise bias.

What you could investigate

  1. Compare tractable distance-three constructions, such as the five-qubit code, Steane seven-qubit code, Shor nine-qubit code, rotated surface-9, and XZZX-9.
  2. Assign syndromes and logical classes to Pauli errors and calculate exact logical-error probabilities across total-error and bias parameters.
  3. Map crossover regions and identify the logical mechanisms responsible for changes in performance.

What you could create

  • Validated exact-enumeration code
  • Crossover maps across noise parameters
  • An explanation of dominant logical-error patterns
