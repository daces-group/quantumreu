Research/Distributed consensus/Q3

Project Q3 · Thrust 03

Consensus with noisy channels and limited resources

Test how channel noise changes consensus, and whether simple protection helps within a fixed interaction budget.

Density matricesNoise modelsResource budgets

The question

Does noise bias the limiting state, impose a convergence floor, or destroy a conserved quantity—and can simple protection improve the result?

Why it matters

Distributed protocols must contend with imperfect communication. This project connects the consensus and coding thrusts by testing whether modest error protection improves collective behavior without changing the research objective.

What you could investigate

  1. Add dephasing, depolarizing, erasure, or measurement errors to a small-network consensus protocol.
  2. Use exact density-matrix simulation to track limiting behavior and distinguish statistical error from model error.
  3. Compare topologies under a fixed interaction budget and test simple error detection, repetition, or a coded communication primitive.

What you could create

  • Validated small-system density-matrix simulations
  • A study of noise, convergence, and resource tradeoffs
  • A comparison of protected and unprotected protocols
